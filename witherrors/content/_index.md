# HOME SWEET HOME

I am witherrors and I am building a Linux first c2 framework. For the first time. In public. Yikes.

## why Linux first? 

Most public c2 tooling is Windows first, has plenty of research, and communities are well established. The Windows space to me feels crowded. I think Linux has a good bit of attack surface now — cloud infra, containers, CI/CD — and it's comparatively underserved. I think there is work to be done. 

## what I am not and for reference:

- A C Programmer (I've worked through [a modern approach](http://knking.com/books/c2/))
- A Rust programmer (I've worked through most of [the rustlang book](https://doc.rust-lang.org/book/))
- No formal cs background (expect sloppyness as we grow)

## what I'm not doing here:
- Using AI for development (no black boxes!)
- Going into evasion (that is <span class="glitch" data-text="MINE">MINE</span>)
- Taking ourselves too seriously (lets have some fun)

## why am I doing this?

- Accountability
- Transparency
- Authenticity

## background:

I have spent professional time as a SOC analyst, a threat hunter, a penetration tester, and as a red teamer. Always a tool user, never a tool developer (I have excuses but no good ones). I need to take the next step. This will be an educational blog of that journey with ALOT of stupidity along the way.

Usually when I come across tools they are in a completed state. Unfortunately, you do not get a glimpse into design decisions, roadblocks, mistakes, etc, unless you've built similar tooling. That is the purpose of this site. To provide a window into that process and if anything else, watch a caveman bang on a keyboard for your amusement.

This project is purely for educational purposes. Only pop your own boxes. This does not reflect views of my employer.
